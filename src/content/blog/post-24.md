# My current social usage
I’d already been pulling away for several years. As a kid I used to be an avid Facebook disciple, planning every day what my status update would be or if I could “poke” that cute upperclassman and get away with it. But times have changed. My feeds are full of ads and ai slop. The political rage baiting was such an issue my therapist and I discussed it multiple times. She often would be the one reminding me that there was no necessity to be on these platforms if they made me upset. I could cull my news consumption down to a daily email check - which I did do a couple years ago. Slowly I’ve been noticing patterns of how social media destroys my life and free time more than it adds to it... and now I want to continue to venture into greater unknown of the small web.

When it comes to posting, I’m really not *that* active. I have the basic accounts moreso to make sure I could lay claim to the name. For a temporary time I was using bluesky and instagram to share quick little updates in projects (you’ll notice some of my WIP pictures are ripped directly from these sites). i don’t think I’ll be deleting these accounts anytime soon, in order to be able to see a lot of the content you need to be logged in. so I’ll continue to use it to get access to those precious little links. But in terms of scrolling through my content feeds I’ve definitely made a decision to try to avoid usage. This will lead into my RSS section later. 

The only social media I currently use is discord and snapchat. These are both primarily focused on messaging and allow me to directly contact people. I honestly would have gotten off of these platforms as well but a lot of friends and family use them. And sadly if I want to see regular updates of my niece tenderly hugging a costco hotdog … I have to be there.

Oh wait… there’s one more I almost forgot about…

# My Strange Addiction: YouTube 
Okay if there’s one vice I still hang onto it’s youtube. This site is my baby. My first love. My golden calf that I worship at the feet of. I understand this site has all the same problems that every other social site has - I’m not a saint. FFS I even had a premium account for awhile. I am part of the problem. [I’m like scooby doo if he wore shoes and he gave money to the enemy FUCK I AM THE ENEMY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GYjuq359VRs&list=RDGYjuq359VRs&start_radio=1). But it’s my goal to not eliminate youtube from my life. Afterall, youtube is where [I first learned about the small web](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yko-LMBrRQw&t=11s") to begin with! But I have some goals to change my habits and usage.

- Block shorts unless I specifically want to see one from a creator, I don’t want to explore them that’s for sure 
- Get better access to creators I’ve subscribed to 
- Categorize my content based on what I’m looking for and what activities they relate to. For this I mean when I’m in the mood to build a miniature, I want to look at my miniature creators

# Building a Better Selection with RSS

So step one of getting away from predatory platforms was to make my own “feed” as it were. In the old days this was done with RSS - a tool I’d recognized but never really paid attention to. Essentially think of this as a feed for any kind of website where every time a page has an update, you get a post about it. 

Now RSS isn’t a website itself - it’s just a method of accessing updates to the website you like. I could go into more detail but i’ll let [the experts](https://unplatform.fromthesuperhighway.com/guide/3) handle that instead. You don’t come here for my lack of programming expertise. You come here for… well … it’s definitely not that. Anyway what we need to get in order to use RSS is a **reader** aka a place where all those updates will populate and we can keep track. 

I know I have some techphobic readers on this blog. I would hate to make you feel like you are not a part of the small web community because you want to scream into the void trying to hyperlink text… if you even know what hyperlinking is. SO - if you want something that functions more like a pinterest widget, I recommend [fraidycat](https://fraidyc.at/). You can download it as an extension on chrome or firefox or as a program on your computer. When I was first exploring RSS this is what I used and it was so simple. And the design is very cute~ 

# Email Renaissance  
When I was a young kid logging onto the family computer after school, I would look forward to opening my AIM chat and yahoo mail the most. Email is such a personalized way to keep in touch yet such a lost art. We’ve turned what was once our sacred space of chain mail messages and 2 liner updates from friends into a sludge puddle of advertisements. 

I started first by completely yeeting out my old emails. My gmails are for work and signing up for stupid accounts. That’s it. My real social time is happening on my [proton](https://proton.me/) accounts. I have one for irl family and friends ONLY. And another for all things small web - subscribing to newsletters, web master things, connecting with strangers. It’s pretty straight forward. I am determined to keep these accounts free from spam. 

# Use it - Sparingly 

So I can’t entirely avoid social media - it’s part of work, part of life. I’ve found many activities, social groups, ideas, and new perspectives from social media that would have never been accessible without its unique abilities. But there’s a phenomenon I’ve found where I think I am having the experience as the content I’m watching. *I* made a dress, *I* wrote an essay, *I* watched a horror movie, *I* solved the bite of ‘87 once and for all…these are things I observe others do and feel a second-hand sense of accomplishment simply for witnessing it. And rather than feeling inspired to do what i just saw, I wait for the auto scroll or next video to pop up and distract me with another serving of content.

In order to get back into doing and away from watching - I installed [screenzen](https://screenzen.co/) to limit the amount of times I even mindlessly open my app as I search for crumbs of brain zappy chemicals. Most of the time I realize that I *don’t* want a video to watch, I just want to stop feeling bored. And with that realization I can step away and do something that will get my juices flowing. 

# Being Intentional with My Time 

Now an app blocker may be enough for you, but if you’re like me you will sometimes fill your hours with endless scrolling knowing it doesn’t satisfy you. But you stay because the idea of having to CHOOSE something else to fill your time sounds more exhausting than just tapping on the little screen. Sometimes I feel like my brain is seriously damaged, other times I feel like the effects of cellphones on human brains were not studied enough, and other times I feel like there is a secret conspiracy of the world oligarchs to reduce our brain’s ability to perform to make us less resistant to their inevitable world domination. It’s definitely at least one of those. 

If there’s one thing I can say for certainty about humans it’s that we’re efficiently lazy. It’s what helps us to survive - do the least amount of work for the most amount of payoff. And we shouldn’t fight this animal instinct - but rather take advantage of it. 

Enter my dopamenu! 

## Dopa-who?

I first heard this term on [How to ADHD](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-6WCkTwW6xg) but it’s evolved to a more global idea. I’ve had to tweak mine over the years to figure out the best way to do it. Some people like to categorize theirs by how much time each activity will take or by areas… not I. My dopamenu has 3 components - restoration, fun, and improvement. And I can only add them on to my day in that order. Doing this allows me to spend freetime in ways that help me recuperate, give me something exciting and new, and also make me feel like I’m making progress towards a goal. 

Now the mechanics of my set up in my obsidian would make for a cool blog post in itself - but for today I’ll limit it to just talking about how social media fits in here. I have playlists correlated to these different menu categories that are part of my dopamenu *specials*. So I pick one video off of these playlists that appeal to me. Saving videos for later in a playlist is also a great way to weed through click bait that you eventually realize you didn’t want to watch. But I also have other things saved on my menu like activities, ideas, links to blogs, locations I want to go, people I want to call - they’re all there as many options. Rather than asking myself the open ended “What do you want to do” I can look through a selection and make a simple choice. Now this doesn’t mean I *HAVE* to only do things on this menu. It’s a tool not a task. But it helps simplify the quest for stimulation in a pinch. 

# In conclusion: make the internet feel real again 
The long fought battle for my sanity online wages on, leaving me feeling like a derealized husk on the worst days and a blubbering chipmunk drinking chemicals out of a lab water dispensor on my best days. I am so aware of the damage that comes from the internet. But I also realize that it was invented this way for a reason - for predatory business men to rob us of every cent by dominating our brain space. The internet and by extension our social apps erode our freewill and thinking. But they just as easily can be re-engineered to do something better. Humans can create extensions, site, and system to make us a better society. 

As my boy [Dr. Vegapunk](https://www.onepiecepodcast.com/opp-magazine/vegapunk-and-einstein-on-moral-relativity) once said "Science is only as good or evil as the person who uses it."
